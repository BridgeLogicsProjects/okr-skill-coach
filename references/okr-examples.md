# OKR Examples by Function

## Product

**Objective:** Make our onboarding experience so clear that new users succeed without needing support

- KR1: Increase 30-day user retention from 40% to 60%
- KR2: Reduce support tickets from new users in first 7 days by 50%
- KR3: Achieve an onboarding NPS of 45 or above

---

**Objective:** Become the most trusted AI-powered tool in our category

- KR1: Increase feature adoption of AI recommendations from 22% to 50% of active users
- KR2: Reduce user-reported errors in AI outputs by 40%
- KR3: Achieve a trust score of 4.5 out of 5 in quarterly user survey

---

## Marketing

**Objective:** Own the go-to-market narrative before the competition defines it for us

- KR1: Increase share of voice in category from 18% to 30% by end of quarter
- KR2: Generate 500 qualified leads from content marketing (up from 210)
- KR3: Achieve a 35% email open rate on product launch campaign

---

## Engineering

**Objective:** Ship with confidence — quality is not a phase, it is a practice

- KR1: Reduce production incidents from 12 per month to 3
- KR2: Increase test coverage from 60% to 85%
- KR3: Achieve a deployment frequency of at least 3 times per week with zero rollbacks

---

## Sales

**Objective:** Turn our best customers into our best sales team

- KR1: Generate 30% of new pipeline from customer referrals (up from 10%)
- KR2: Increase net revenue retention from 95% to 110%
- KR3: Close 5 expansion deals from existing accounts this quarter

---

## AI Product (AI PM specific)

**Objective:** Build AI features users actually trust enough to act on

- KR1: Increase AI-assisted decision adoption rate from 15% to 40%
- KR2: Reduce user override of AI recommendations by 30% (a signal of trust)
- KR3: Complete responsible AI review for all new model integrations before launch — 100% compliance

---

## Notes on Reading These Examples

- Every Key Result has a baseline and a target. If you do not know the baseline, finding it is your first task.
- None of these describe shipping a feature. They describe what changes because of the feature.
- Confidence should sit between 6 and 7 out of 10. These are not guarantees. They are commitments.
